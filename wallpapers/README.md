# Wallpapers

Four designs, each in a light and a dark version, 3840×2160. The SVG files are the sources; the PNG files are rendered from them.

| Design | Files | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strata | `roxora-strata-{light,dark}` | The mark's three bands spread into faceted layers. No text. |
| Strata with wordmark | `roxora-strata-wordmark-{light,dark}` | Strata with a small mark and "roxora" in the empty area at the top left. |
| Facet | `roxora-facet-{light,dark}` | The mark and wordmark on the left, a large low-contrast mark cropped at the right. |
| Bands | `roxora-bands-{light,dark}` | Three flat bands in the mark's colours across the lower half. |

Colours are the ones in the main README. The wordmark is Space Grotesk Bold, converted to outlines, so the SVG files do not need the font installed.

Roxora's images include them through a `roxora-wallpapers` package. On a server image nothing shows; once a GNOME desktop is installed, all four are in the background list in Settings and Facet is the default, light or dark with the desktop's style.

Made by [DavidQyn](https://github.com/DavidQyn) with Ga Hing Woo.
